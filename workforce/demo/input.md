# Demo Input

The single input to paste into the Hormozi GTM Strategist workforce for the Loom demo.

## The input

> We launched a new tier of our SaaS product at $2K/month base + usage. 40 demos in the last 8 weeks. Conversion to paid is 7.5%. Prospects say they love the product on the call, but most go quiet after. The two objections we hear most: "we need internal alignment first" and "let's see how Q2 budgets land." Marketing wants to add more case studies. Sales wants to drop the base fee. What's actually broken?

## Why this input

It's generic B2B SaaS (no Immutable-specific context required) but specific enough to be diagnosable:

- **Specific numbers:** $2K/month, 40 demos, 8 weeks, 7.5% conversion. Gives the workforce something to score.
- **The trap:** the surface symptoms point to sales (objections) and pricing (sales wants to drop the base fee). The Triage Agent must see past that to the Offer-level Perceived Likelihood gap.
- **Real internal politics:** marketing wants one thing, sales wants another. This is what real GTM problems look like — competing intuitions inside the team.
- **A clear "right answer":** the workforce should route to the Offer Specialist and recommend risk reversal (performance guarantee, comparable-customer references, demo restructure) over the obvious moves (more case studies, lower price).

## The demo's narrative beat

When Aden pastes this and watches the workforce respond, the moment of "oh that's clever" is when the Triage Agent says: *"This looks like a sales or pricing problem on the surface — sales wants to drop the price, marketing wants more case studies. But the symptoms point upstream. Routing to the Offer Specialist."*

That's the "the first agent refuses to answer — it diagnoses first" beat.
